🇨🇴 News about the Satanath Records (https://t.me/satanath) festival! We tell you about the Espías Malignos (https://youtu.be/D5RRYAqsXTI), who will perform in both cities!

After 2 international tours in Europe, as well as shows in Russia & Morocco, band is preparing for their 3rd tour! Project will start in Armenia & Georgia, where they will perform for the first time! At the end of 2023, they had the honor of opening for Sodom, and in 1999 (under the name Darkness), guys shared the stage with Metallica. In Colombia, they participated in large open-air fests, in 2024 they played with Crisix at Sua Rock Fest. Band was founded in 2020, uniting 2 former members of Darkness, which existed since 1986. Album "Oscura", produced by famous Waldemar Sorychta (Grip Inc, together with Dave Lombardo), was released in 2021. Musicians play driving and assertive thrash metal with elements of groove/death metal...
