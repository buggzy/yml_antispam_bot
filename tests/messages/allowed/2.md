News about the Satanath Records (https://t.me/satanath) music fest! We tell you about the fourth band Exitus Letalis (https://youtu.be/fyULfgih6V4), which will perform in both cities!

EXITUS LETALIS ("Летальный Исход") is an old school death metal project from Zlatoust, the heart of the Urals. This year, the band will celebrate its fifth anniversary. During this time, the formation managed to record two full-length albums (one of which was released on Satanath Records) and play at several iconic festivals of the country, including "Death-Panorama" and "In the Middle of Cemeteries and Swamps". At the moment, ЛЕТАЛЬНЫЙ ИСХОД is working in the studio on a co-release with another famous Ural death metal band, which will be published this fall...
